Progression and recovery

Block length4 weeks. Weeks 1–3 build, week 4 deloads at 60–65% for the same sets and reps, then the next block starts from the new weights.
Weekly jumps+2.5 kg squat and deadlift, +1.25–2.5 kg bench, only when every rep is completed with good bar speed.
Missed repsTwo weeks running on the same lift: hold the weight for a week rather than dropping it.
AccessoriesAdd a rep first, then a small weight jump once you hit the top of the range on every set.
Rest5+ min on the heavy compound sets (deadlift 5×3, squat 5×5, bench 5×5 and 5×3). 2–3 min on the lighter barbell work, 90 s on accessories.
MobilityWarm-up is mandatory before every session. Stretching goes after lifting or on rest days, never before heavy sets. Rest-day session: full stretch list, 15–20 min.
ConditioningConversational pace only. Never a hard session the day before a lower day.

Percentages are of your current 1RM. Retest at the end of each block.
